## Three-step significance review — can an outside reader answer "So what?"

- **I am studying:** Is the topic specific?
- **because I want to find out:** Is the question genuinely unresolved, testable and worth knowing?
- **in order to help my reader understand:** Who benefits, and what broader understanding or decision changes?
- **Adversarial check:** Is the significance only "improve accuracy", "build a system", prestige, or another author-centered motive? If so, REVISE.

## Reader's five argument challenges

1. **Claim — What is your point?** ...
2. **Reasons — Why should I believe it?** ...
3. **Evidence — How do you know?** Recompute critical estimates and identify sources/run IDs/hashes; unsupported or missing findings are not evidence.
4. **Acknowledgement & Response — But what about this other idea?** Present the strongest alternative fairly, including findings that contradict the desired account.
5. **Warrant — How does the "why" connect to your point?** State exactly why the evidence and reasons support *this claim* rather than a weaker alternative.
