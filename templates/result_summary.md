## Five-part claim-to-evidence map

**Claim:** What exactly do these results warrant?

**Reasons:** Which mechanism or observations support that specific claim?

**Evidence:** Inspectable result paths/hash/primary sources; no source-free inference.

**Acknowledgement & Response:** Most credible competing explanation; what the data do and do not resolve.

**Warrant:** Why do these reasons and evidence imply the scoped claim? Where might the inference fail?

**Reader significance:** How does the answer improve someone's understanding or decision, beyond advancing the author's work?

**Evidence/novelty/publication gates:** independent human review required.
