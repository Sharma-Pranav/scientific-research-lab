# Research proposal

## Three-step formula for reader significance ("So what?")

1. **I am studying** — name the concrete topic.
2. **because I want to find out** — state the specific unknown.
3. **in order to help my reader understand** — explain what others will be better able to understand or decide.

Complete the corresponding sections in **significance.md**. Do not confuse an interesting technique, a usable dataset, or a performance improvement with reader significance.

## The reader's five argument questions

- **Claim — What is your point?** State a scoped, falsifiable conclusion.
- **Reasons — Why should I believe it?** Explain why the claim follows from the proposed mechanism.
- **Evidence — How do you know?** Link inspectable sources, observed results or a proof. Pre-experiment evidence is a plan, not an observation.
- **Acknowledgement & Response — What about this other idea?** Address the strongest credible counterargument.
- **Warrant — Why does the "why" connect to the point?** Make the inference between reasons/evidence and claim explicit; name scope and uncertainty.
