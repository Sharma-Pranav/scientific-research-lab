# Independent Review

Using raw artifact IDs, protocol and evidence matrix only, recompute metrics when possible; inspect gates and validity. First test the three-step significance statement against the reader's "So what?": topic,
specific question, and what the answer helps a reader understand or decide.

Then answer the reader's five distinct questions without collapsing them:
(1) What is your point? — Claim;
(2) Why should I believe it? — Reasons;
(3) How do you know? — inspectable Evidence;
(4) But what about the strongest other explanation? — Acknowledgement & Response;
(5) Why do these reasons and evidence support the claim? — Warrant.
Use result artifact hashes and primary sources, and challenge unsupported inferences,
weak reader impact, selection bias and missing counterarguments.

Produce separate structural-completeness and human scientific GO/REVISE/STOP decisions,
plus boundaries and limitations. Passing preflight never establishes scientific truth. Reject fabricated results and unsupported novelty.

Deliver a concrete artifact or table, not only a persuasive narrative. Explicitly separate observed evidence, hypothesis and human judgment. Cite sources by stable URI and run evidence by file path/hash.
